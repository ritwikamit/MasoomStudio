# Design System

## Brand character
Cinematic, intimate, handcrafted and story-led. Use the supplied logo as the visual source of truth.

## Suggested colors
- Ink black `#050505`
- Deep charcoal `#111111`
- Surface `#191715`
- Warm white `#F7F3EE`
- White `#FFFFFF`
- Champagne gold `#C9A16A`
- Antique gold `#A97B48`
- Muted text `#C9C2B8`

Gold is an accent for fine lines, selected labels and CTA details, not the dominant color of every component.

## Typography
Use an elegant editorial serif such as Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display for headings; a clean sans-serif such as Manrope or Inter for body/UI; and a restrained script font for occasional accents. Never use typewriter fonts or script for long paragraphs.

## Logo
Use `logo/masoom-studio-logo.svg` for scalable display and compare with the supplied JPG. The SVG uses selectable script text with fallback fonts; exact appearance may vary by device. For pixel-perfect fidelity, use the original logo image or convert licensed font glyphs to paths during a vector-production pass.

## Layout and motion
Large authentic imagery, generous spacing, editorial captions, restrained transitions. Avoid heavy cards around every image, excessive gold outlines, circles and dots. Respect reduced motion and preserve readable content when animations are disabled.
