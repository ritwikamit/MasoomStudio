# PRD – Masoom Studio

## Summary
Create an elegant, cinematic portfolio and enquiry website for a Patna-based wedding photography and filmmaking studio. Showcase authentic portfolio work, explain confirmed services, and make it easy to enquire about event dates.

## Goals
- Establish a strong official digital presence.
- Showcase real photography and film work.
- Explain confirmed services and creative approach.
- Convert visitors into phone/WhatsApp enquiries or form submissions.
- Follow the supplied black, white and champagne-gold logo identity.
- Support local discovery in Patna and outstation enquiries if confirmed.

## Audience
Couples planning weddings, families arranging engagement/sangeet/roka/reception, and clients looking for pre-wedding shoots or films.

## Proposed pages
Home, About/Our Approach, Weddings, Pre-Wedding, Films, Portfolio/Gallery, Other Functions (if confirmed), Contact/Check Your Date, Privacy Policy. Only create standalone pages with real content and images.

## Features
Cinematic hero with approved media; real portfolio gallery; film showcase; confirmed service list; enquiry form; phone/WhatsApp actions; social links; responsive layout; accessible lightbox and motion controls.

## Content safeguards
The supplied logo says “ESTB. 2018”, while the public vendor listing says wedding work began in 2019. Confirm before writing a founding-date claim. Do not publish third-party prices, timelines, payment terms, cancellation policies, testimonials or awards as official facts without owner approval.

## Out of scope unless approved
Payments, booking engine, client login/private gallery, CRM/database, copied public prices, automated scraping of social profiles.

## Acceptance
Mobile-first responsive design; approved portfolio assets; verified contact/social links; validated form and spam controls; unique SEO metadata; accessible motion; successful build, link checks and accessibility smoke tests.
